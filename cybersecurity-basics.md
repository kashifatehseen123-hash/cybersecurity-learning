## What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, and data from unauthorized access, attacks, and security threats.

## Important Concepts

### 1. Network Security

Protecting computer networks from unauthorized access and attacks.

### 2. Vulnerability

A weakness in a system that could potentially be exploited.

### 3. Threat

A potential event or activity that could cause harm to a system or its data.

### 4. Authentication

The process of verifying the identity of a user or system.

### 5. Authorization

Determining what an authenticated user is allowed to access.
